Drop your songs here (.mp3 or .wav — keep filenames lowercase,
spaces/symbols out, e.g. our_song.mp3).

Then open lib/content.dart -> finalePlaylist and add one
PlaylistTrack per song, like:

  PlaylistTrack(
    title: 'Your Song',
    artist: 'Artist Name',
    audioAsset: 'music/your_song.mp3',
    coverAsset: 'music/cover.jpg',          // optional artwork
    duration: Duration(minutes: 3, seconds: 45), // optional
  ),

Remember to only bundle music you're allowed to share.
demo_melody.wav is a generated placeholder — delete it whenever.
